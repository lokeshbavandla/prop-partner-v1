About us

One partner, accountable for every stage of project success.

The Prop Partner is a specialised real estate advisory, mandate sales and project lifecycle management company — we partner with developers from the earliest stages of conception through to handover, so they work with a single strategic partner across the whole journey.

Our philosophy

Developers deserve more than a vendor. They deserve a strategic partner capable of guiding a project from concept to completion.

Successful real estate projects are not created by marketing alone. They're built through the seamless integration of market intelligence, product planning, branding, sales strategy, customer experience and execution.
