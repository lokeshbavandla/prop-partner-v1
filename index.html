The problem

Great projects rarely fail at sales. They fail at coordination.

Most developers run a project through six vendors: one for research, another for branding, a digital agency, a sales team or two, a handful of open broker mandates, and a CRM nobody really owns. Each does its piece. None owns the result.

When bookings stall, every vendor points at the one before it, and the developer carries the risk alone. The work that decides whether a project sells happens before any of them arrive.

The questions developers actually ask.

You're a young firm. What's your track record?

We don't have a twenty-year scoreboard. We have a method built to earn one, and relationships that show it's working.

Why an exclusive mandate instead of several open brokers?

Open mandates split incentives: every broker competes on price and nobody owns the strategy. An exclusive mandate lets one accountable team set pricing, control the narrative, manage the channel network, and protect your project's positioning, while still bringing the full breadth of brokers to the table under a single plan.

How is an engagement structured and priced?

It depends on scope and stage. We typically work on a combination of a mandate fee and success-linked component, so our upside is tied to your project's results. We'll walk through the exact structure for your project in the first conversation. There's no standard rate card, because no two mandates are the same.

We already have a sales team. Why bring you in?

Plenty of our developers do. We don't replace a good team, we give it a strategy, a channel network, and the upstream research and positioning that decide whether the team has an easy sell or a hard one. Where it helps, we strengthen the team; where you'd rather we run it end-to-end, we can.

Do we have to engage you for all eight stages?

No. You can take the full lifecycle, or engage us for a single stage, say research and product strategy, or sales and channel management. The benefit of single-point accountability is strongest end-to-end, but the model is built to fit where your project actually is.

Which cities and project types do you work on?

Hyderabad and Bangalore today, across residential, villa, plotted, and mixed-use developments. If your project sits in those markets and at any stage from ideation to in-sales, it's worth a conversation.
