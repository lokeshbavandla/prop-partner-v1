Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Template — review with legal counsel before launch.

The Prop Partner ("we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry form, we collect the details you provide — such as your name, company, role, phone number, email, city and information about your project. We may also collect basic, non-identifying analytics about how the site is used.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation.
  • To understand your project and prepare relevant proposals.
  • To improve our website and services.

We process this data on the basis of your consent, which you provide when you submit a form.

How we store and share it

Your data is stored securely and accessed only by team members who need it to respond to you. We do not sell your data. We may share it with service providers (for example, email or CRM tools) strictly to operate our business, under appropriate safeguards.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may withdraw consent at any time, by writing to hello@thepropartner.in.

Retention

We retain enquiry data only as long as necessary to respond to and serve your request, or as required by law.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact us at hello@thepropartner.in.
